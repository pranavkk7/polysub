PolySub

Subtitles in any language, timed to the word.

PolySub recognises the speech in a video with the model that measured best for that language, builds subtitles that follow professional reading rules, and translates them with Claude. Below is real output it produced for a Japanese clip.

The real transcripts (FLEURS reference)

    Making this demo found a bug: Whisper skipped sentence 2 in the middle of its 30-second window. PolySub now recognises any detected speech that produced no words a second time, on its own. On 90 FLEURS clips the error rate stayed the same (3.85%), and no text appeared in long silences.
